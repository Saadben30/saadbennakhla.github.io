About Me

I'm a 20-year-old IT student based in Morocco, currently pursuing my degree in Digital Infrastructures with a specialization in Systems & Networks at OFPPT. My passion lies in understanding how networks operate, securing digital systems, and exploring the fascinating world of virtualization and Linux environments.

As a natural leader, I serve as the group leader in my class, where I help coordinate team projects and support my peers. I'm fluent in Arabic (Darija), French, and English, which allows me to collaborate effectively in diverse environments.

My goal is to specialize in cybersecurity engineering, where I can combine my technical skills with my problem-solving abilities to protect organizations from digital threats. I'm constantly learning and experimenting with new technologies to stay ahead in this rapidly evolving field.
